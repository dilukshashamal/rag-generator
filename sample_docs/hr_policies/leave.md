# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
Employees receive 20 days of annual leave per calendar year.
Annual leave accrues monthly at one twelfth of the annual entitlement.
Submit annual leave requests at least 10 working days before the start date.
The line manager must approve annual leave before travel is booked.
Up to 5 unused annual leave days may carry over until March 31 of the following year.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick leave days per calendar year.
Notify the line manager before 9 AM on the first day of sickness.
A medical certificate is required after 3 consecutive sick leave days.

## Parental Leave
Eligible primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave.
Eligible secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave.
Parental leave eligibility requires 12 months of continuous service.
Submit a parental leave request to HR at least 8 weeks before the expected start date.

## Unpaid Leave
Unpaid leave requires written approval from both the line manager and HR.
Annual leave does not accrue during unpaid leave lasting more than 30 consecutive days.
