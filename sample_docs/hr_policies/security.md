# Information Security Policy

## Account Security
Multi-factor authentication is required on all company accounts.
Never share account passwords with colleagues or external parties.
Report suspected phishing to the IT security team immediately.

## Data Handling
Store confidential company documents only in approved company storage.
Personal email accounts must not be used to send company documents.
Lock your screen whenever you leave your workstation unattended.

## Incident Response
Report suspected data exposure to the IT security team within 1 hour of discovery.
Do not delete evidence or investigate a suspected breach independently.

## Remote Access
Use a company-managed device and the company VPN when accessing internal systems remotely.
Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the company VPN active.
