# Workplace Policy

## Working Hours
The standard working week is 40 hours from Monday to Friday.
Core collaboration hours are 10 AM to 3 PM in the employee's contracted time zone.
Overtime must receive written manager approval before the work is performed.

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with line manager approval.
Employees must remain in their contracted country while working remotely.
International remote work is not permitted under this policy.
Use a company-managed device and the company VPN when accessing internal systems remotely.

## Probation
The probation period is 6 months.
The line manager conducts probation reviews at months 3 and 6.

## Conduct
Report harassment to HR or the confidential ethics channel.
Retaliation against employees who report concerns in good faith is prohibited.
