# Benefits and Development Policy

## Health Benefits
Company health coverage starts on the first day of the month following the employee's start date.
Eligible dependents are a spouse or partner and children under 21 years old.
Employees must notify HR of a dependent change within 30 calendar days.

## Learning Budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1000 currency units.
Learning purchases require line manager approval before enrollment.
Unused learning budget does not carry forward into the next calendar year.

## Equipment
Employees receive one company-managed laptop for work.
Report lost company equipment to IT within 1 hour of discovering the loss.
Return company equipment by the last working day when leaving the company.
