# Expenses and Travel Policy

## Expense Claims
Submit expense claims within 30 calendar days of the expense date.
Attach an itemized receipt for every expense claim.
Approved expense claims are reimbursed in the next monthly payroll.

## Business Travel
Business travel requires line manager approval before booking.
Economy class is required for all flights under 6 hours.
The domestic hotel limit is 150 currency units per night, excluding taxes.
The domestic meal allowance is 40 currency units per day.
Personal entertainment expenses are not reimbursable.
